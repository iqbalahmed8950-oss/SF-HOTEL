Midwester US-Fish

Freshwater fish are a significant part of the Midwest US diet and culture, particularly in the Upper Midwest where species like walleye, perch, and crappie are highly prized.

Southeastern US-fish

The Southeastern United States is a global hotspot for freshwater biodiversity, containing the richest freshwater fish fauna on the North American continent north of Mexico.

Pescado Frito(Spain)-Fish

Pescado frito (also known as pescaíto frito) is a traditional Spanish dish of fried fish originating from the southern coast, particularly Andalusia, though it is also popular in Catalonia, Valencia, and the Balearic Islands

Sole Meunière (France)-fish

Sole meunière is a classic French dish consisting of sole (typically Dover sole or lemon sole) that is lightly dredged in flour and pan-fried in butter until golden.

Ikan Goreng (Indonesia)-fish

kan goreng is a popular Southeast Asian dish, particularly in Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, and Brunei, consisting of fish or seafood that is marinated in spices and deep-fried until golden and crispy.

Lamb(Sliced Gyros)

Toasted lamb gyros are typically made by baking seasoned ground lamb into a loaf, slicing it thinly, and then broiling or pan-frying the slices until the edges are crispy and browned. This method replicates the texture of meat cooked on a vertical rotisserie, offering a contrast between the crispy exterior and the juicy interior.
